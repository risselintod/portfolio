About Me

Passionate developer with 3+ years of experience

Full-Stack Developer Based in Manila

Hi! I'm Rissel Intod, a Full-Stack Developer with over 3 years of experience building clean, responsive, and user-friendly web and mobile applications. My sweet spot is Front-End and Mobile development, and I specialize in the MERN stack, ReactJS, and React Native.

I love transforming ideas into real solutions that make people's lives easier. For example, I built and published customer and admin apps for AP Cargo, a leading logistics provider. In that project, I turned Figma designs into smooth, functional interfaces and worked closely with designers and stakeholders to deliver a great user experience.

I thrive on learning, teamwork, and solving problems — and I love taking projects from idea to live launch, including real-world publishing for iOS and Android. Right now, I'm open to Full-Stack, Front-End, or Mobile Developer roles where I can grow, contribute, and make a positive impact.
